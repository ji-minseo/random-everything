Random Team Generator

Paste a list of names and split everyone into shuffled teams without spreadsheets or arguments.

Names are shuffled locally in your browser.

Future ad space

How teams are made

Paste the names, choose how many teams you need and click Make teams. The list is shuffled before people are distributed across the teams.

Useful for groups

Random teams work well for classrooms, workshops, sports, party games and casual events where you want a quick neutral split.

Runs in your browser

The names you enter are processed locally. The tool does not require an account or upload your list to create the teams.

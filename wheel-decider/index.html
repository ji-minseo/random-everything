How to use the wheel

Replace the sample choices with your own items and press Spin the wheel. Each listed item gets an equal slice of the wheel.

When a wheel is useful

Use it for classroom picks, party games, meal decisions, chores, names or any choice where the visual spin makes the result more fun.

Fast and local

The wheel is created directly in your browser, so you can change the list and spin again immediately without creating an account.
